Put your face on your badge

Type your handles, plug in your Carolina Code Conference badge, and this page writes the whole thing — photo, QR codes, libraries and code — straight to it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; it all happens in your browser.

Your details

Your browser can't write to the badge directly — that needs the File System Access API, which today means Chrome or Edge on the desktop. Use Download .zip and unzip it onto the CIRCUITPY drive (keep the folder structure), or run flash.py from the repo.
What your badge will show

    How to use it: plug the badge in over USB and wait for the CIRCUITPY drive to mount. Hit Build preview, check it looks right, then Write to badge… and pick that drive when the folder chooser opens. About 78 KB gets written and the badge restarts on its own.

    Once it's running: SW1 and SW2 step through the sides, SW3 toggles the LEDs to save battery. Your details end up in badge_profile.py on the drive — edit it in any text editor, save, and CircuitPython reloads instantly.
